How do I restore Premium?

Open the App, tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose Celebration Candles & Cakes and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription; you keep it until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I blow out the candles?

During a celebration, tap a flame to blow out that candle, or tap the screen and choose Blow out to put them all out. Choose Relight for another wish.

The cake is too big or in the wrong place

While a celebration is playing, drag to move the cake and candles and pinch to resize them. Use − and + to zoom, or Fit to screen (or Re-center) to go back to the automatic position. The Flame slider changes the flame size.

Can I move or remove a candle while it is playing?

Yes. Drag a candle to move it, or tap it and choose Delete candle. Your changes are kept with the scene when you exit.

How do I send a cake to someone?

Tap Share in the editor, on Home or while a celebration is playing. The link opens the cake in any web browser at celebration.chowchowlabs.com, with no app or sign-in needed. The scene travels inside the link and is not stored on our servers.

How do I stop children from leaving the celebration?

Tap Lock in the controls; hold anywhere for 3 seconds to unlock. For a full lock, use Guided Access in your device’s Accessibility settings.

Will I lose my scenes if I reinstall?

Saved scenes are stored only on your device. Deleting the App removes them.

How do I turn off usage statistics?

Open Settings in the App and turn off Share anonymous usage stats under Privacy. See the Privacy Policy for what is collected.
